Hello, my name is Yiwa, welcome! 

Starting from February 2025, I am giving a series of Maths Flash Talks at Rockford Manor School. The goal is to show the fun side of maths, convince more students that it is not boring, and to encourage more girls to pursue a STEM careers. The talks are meant to be accessible to even 1st year students (~12 year olds), but entertaining to all. 

You can find the notes to the talks here on this website, published every Wednesday. Please email if you have any comments, or if I made any mistakes.

This website is built using Jekyll. I have wanted to make a website for some time, and when I was starting this project I knew I really needed to make one. I have heard good things about Jekyll, and thought, why not take this opportunity to learn to do it, and also finally learn to use GitHub?

Oh what a pain it was. I am not very good with technology to begin with and building this website gave me such a headache. It took me forever to get to this very primitive state. I'll be adding more updates once I learn more, but hopefully it is functional for now.
